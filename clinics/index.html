Questions clinics ask

What does Certified mean?

Certified means dosego.org has verified that the clinic is present and listed on the platform. It is not a paid placement.

What does Promoted change?

Promoted adds priority visibility, expanded profile content, and a Promoted badge across eligible map and list views.

How does payment work?

Promoted participation is offered through a transparent installment plan. We confirm available terms for your location during onboarding.

Does Promoted affect medical care or certification?

No. Promoted is a visibility program. Clinical suitability, licensing, and treatment decisions remain between the traveler and the clinic.
