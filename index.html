Questions people ask before traveling

Does dosego.org arrange guest dosing?

No. dosego.org helps you find and organize clinics. You must contact the clinic and your home program to confirm guest-dosing arrangements.

Can I plan multiple cities?

Yes. Add every destination and its dose dates, then choose a clinic for each stop. USA Only.

Is my information private?

The planning interface is designed to work without an account. No data is stored or shared.
