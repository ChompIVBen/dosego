Treatment Assistance

For information about substance-use treatment and referral services in the United States, contact the SAMHSA National Helpline. It offers free, confidential information and referrals.

1-800-662-HELP (4357) Visit SAMHSA ↗
♡

Crisis Support

In the United States, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline for free, confidential support. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Call or text 988 Visit 988 Lifeline ↗

Narcotics Anonymous (NA)

NA offers peer-support meetings for people seeking recovery from drug addiction. Many meetings are free to attend, with in-person and online options available. Participation is voluntary.
